2. What GharKhoj Is

GharKhoj is an online property listing platform that allows landlords, property owners, and agents ("Listers") to post property listings, and tenants and buyers ("Seekers") to browse and contact Listers directly. GharKhoj is not a real estate broker and does not participate in or facilitate transactions between users.

3. Listing Rules

By posting a listing, you confirm that:

  • You are the owner or authorised representative of the property
  • All information provided is accurate and not misleading
  • The property is genuinely available at the time of posting
  • You will remove or update the listing when it is no longer available
  • You will not post duplicate, spam, or fake listings
  • The listing does not violate any Nepalese law

4. Prohibited Content

The following are strictly prohibited on GharKhoj:
