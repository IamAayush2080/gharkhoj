Fixing Nepal's Broken Property Market

GharKhoj was built because finding a home in Nepal shouldn't require a middleman, a Facebook scroll, or a broker fee.

Our Mission

Nepal's Trusted Property Search Platform

In Nepal, finding a flat, room, or land to buy meant relying on brokers, word of mouth, and unreliable Facebook posts. Families paid commissions just to get information. Students moving to Kathmandu had nowhere reliable to look. NRNs wanting to invest from abroad had no trustworthy platform.

GharKhoj exists to change that. We built a clean, mobile-first, bilingual platform where landlords post directly, tenants search freely, and nobody pays a middleman for basic information.

We believe property search in Nepal should be as easy and trustworthy as anywhere in the world.
