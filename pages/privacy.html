Privacy Policy

How GharKhoj collects, uses, and protects your information

Last updated: June 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you post a listing on GharKhoj, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address, and property details. When you browse listings, we collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) to improve our service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To publish your property listing on GharKhoj
  • To allow interested tenants/buyers to contact you directly
  • To verify listings and prevent fraud
  • To send important service updates (no spam)
  • To improve our platform based on usage patterns

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Your contact information (phone/WhatsApp) is displayed publicly on your listing so potential tenants and buyers can reach you directly. This is the core function of the service — please only submit a phone number you are comfortable sharing.

4. Cookies

We use minimal cookies to remember your language preference (English or Nepali) and to understand how visitors use the site via anonymous analytics. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Our site is served over HTTPS. We do not store passwords (no account system currently). Listing data is stored securely.

6. Your Rights

You may request removal of your listing and associated personal data at any time by emailing info@gharkhoj.com.np. We will process removal requests within 72 hours.

7. Children's Privacy

GharKhoj is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our service evolves. Significant changes will be announced on the site. Continued use of GharKhoj after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Email us at info@gharkhoj.com.np.
